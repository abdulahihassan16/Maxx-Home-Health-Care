About us · Rochester, Minnesota

About Maxx Home Healthcare

Helping you maintain your independence and dignity at home.

Vision

At Maxx Home Healthcare LLC, our vision is to create a world where individuals of all abilities have the opportunity to live independently, thrive in their communities, and achieve their highest potential. We are committed to empowering our clients by providing compassionate, person-centered care that enhances well-being, fosters independence, and strengthens essential life skills.

Mission

At Maxx Home Healthcare LLC, we are dedicated to providing exceptional, person-centered home healthcare services that empower individuals to live independently while receiving the support they need. Our mission is to enhance the quality of life for seniors and individuals with disabilities by delivering compassionate, high-quality, and customized care tailored to each person’s unique needs.

Core Values

At Maxx Home Health Care LLC, our core values are the foundation of everything we do—shaping the care we provide through our Comprehensive Home Care Services and CFSS (Community First Services and Supports) programs.

We are dedicated to delivering compassionate, client-centered care that promotes independence, dignity, and well-being for every individual we serve. Our team values cultural sensitivity, professionalism, and reliability, ensuring that each client feels respected, understood, and supported in the comfort of their home. Through collaboration, integrity, and accountability, we strive to make a lasting difference, helping our clients live safely, confidently, and with purpose in their homes and communities. At Maxx Home Health Care, care is more than a service; it’s a commitment to improving lives with heart, respect, and excellence.

Our Story

Maxx Home Healthcare was created from a deep passion to serve others with dignity, care, and compassion. Our journey began with a simple belief: that every person deserves to feel safe, supported, and valued in their own home. Inspired by real experiences in the healthcare world, we saw just how much of a difference it makes when care is not only professional, but personal.

Helping You Feel at Home, Wherever You Are

From personalized home support to assistance with daily living, we’re here to guide and care for you every step of the way.

If you’re the one receiving care, this part is for you.

You know your own routine better than anyone. You know how you like your coffee, when you like to rest, and what you’d rather do yourself.

Our job isn’t to take over. It’s to help with the parts that have gotten harder, so you can keep doing the parts you love. You have a say in who comes into your home, when they come, and what they help with.

If something isn’t working, tell us. We’ll fix it.
